How it works

Four quick steps from pixels to a sentence.

1

Upload & validate

Type, size and dimensions are checked, then the image is converted to RGB.

2

Vision encoder

A Vision Transformer slices the picture into patches and encodes what is in it.

3

Text decoder

A language decoder writes the caption word by word using beam search.

4

Clean & show

The text is tidied, capitalised and typed out in the result card.
